# I Care Group Services — Website Redesign Prompt

Prepared 14 October 2026. Copy the prompt below into your website design or development tool.

---

Act as a senior web designer, accessibility specialist, NDIS copywriter and local SEO strategist. Redesign the complete website for **I Care Group Services**, a Melbourne disability support provider, into a polished, premium, welcoming and accessible website.

Existing website: https://icgservices.com.au/index.html

Use the current website as the factual starting point. **Keep the original logo and established colour scheme.** Create a substantially improved layout, navigation, content hierarchy and participant experience. Premium should mean considered design, clarity, quality imagery and reassuring detail, with a warm and inclusive tone.

Work in a separate review version. Keep the existing public website available until the replacement is approved for launch.

## 3. Audience and purpose

Design for NDIS participants, families, carers, support coordinators, plan managers and other referrers. Make it easy to:

- Understand the support available and decide whether it may suit their needs.
- Learn how the organisation approaches choice, independence, safety and inclusion.
- Speak with the team, request a callback or make a referral.
- Find practical contact, feedback and policy information quickly.

Use Australian English and respectful, participant-led language. Address the reader directly. Explain unfamiliar NDIS terminology. Focus on practical support and personal goals rather than exaggerated promises or institutional language.

Primary action: **Talk to Our Team**

2. A concise explanation of the organisation's approach, with factual trust signals.
3. An easy-to-scan services overview, grouped around everyday needs.
4. A short section about individual goals, choice and culturally respectful support.
5. A simple getting-started journey: contact the team, discuss needs and suitability, agree on next steps. Confirm this reflects the actual intake process.
6. A preview of the consolidated About page and its promises, principles and safety information.
7. Helpful questions about contacting the team, referrals and exploring support options. Keep funding answers within verified information.
8. A clear contact section and complete footer.

Do not fabricate testimonials or statistics to fill space. Include real reviews or stories only where the business can supply permission and evidence. Replace the automatic hero slider with a single, well-composed introduction unless multiple slides serve a clear user need.

## 8. Contact, referral and feedback experiences

Make the contact page immediately useful: address, click-to-call number, email, published hours, a directions link and an optional lightweight map. Do not let a map delay access to contact details.

Keep general enquiries short: name, preferred contact method, relevant contact details, support interest and message. Explain what happens after submission without inventing response-time promises.

Reorganise the existing referral form into manageable steps with progress, clear labels, optional fields where appropriate and helpful error messages. Support self-referrals and referrals by another person. Preserve genuinely necessary intake information and consent requirements, while reviewing the need and timing for sensitive health or behaviour questions. Do not require extensive clinical details for a basic enquiry.

Explain how submitted information will be used. Use an appropriately protected submission workflow; do not put sensitive referral content in URLs, analytics events or browser storage. Include accessible validation, error recovery and an honest success state.

For implementation, verify real form delivery with dummy data. The current site references PHP form handlers; if the technology changes, implement supported replacements and test them. Do not retain non-working form actions or simulate successful delivery. Treat enquiry, referral and feedback routes as distinct journeys.

## 9. Accessibility, mobile use and performance

Target **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** and test the complete user journeys. Include semantic headings, descriptive links, keyboard navigation, visible focus, a skip link, meaningful image alternatives, form labels and accessible status messages. Check contrast, zoom, narrow-screen reflow and reduced-motion preferences. Make touch targets comfortably sized and prevent sticky elements from covering content or controls.

Use clear, legible text and short sections to reduce cognitive effort. Provide captions or equivalent accessible content for retained videos. Do not rely on an accessibility overlay to resolve underlying problems.

Optimise responsive images, reserve image dimensions, lazy-load below-the-fold media, limit unnecessary scripts and avoid autoplay media. Check real mobile layouts as well as desktop. Keep the implementation compatible with the confirmed hosting environment and avoid a technology migration solely for visual changes.

## 10. Melbourne SEO and launch continuity

Build useful local relevance around the actual Niddrie location and Melbourne context. Use natural language such as NDIS support in Melbourne and disability support in Niddrie where it fits. Create suburb pages only when the business genuinely serves those areas and has distinct, useful information to provide.

Provide unique page titles and descriptions, a clear main heading, descriptive URLs, relevant internal links, an XML sitemap, correct canonicals and appropriate structured data containing only visible, verified business information. Do not add invented review ratings or service locations.

Inventory existing public URLs and map each changed address to its closest relevant replacement. Include the four About redirects above and canonicalise the homepage consistently. Keep the review version out of search results; check production indexing settings as part of an approved launch.

## 11. Required result

Deliver a complete, consistent redesign with:

- The original logo and recognisable blue-and-green brand retained.
- A refined desktop and mobile design system applied across all pages.
- Clear rewritten page copy and all verified contact information.
- One complete About page combining the four specified source pages.
- Useful service information, contact routes, referral and feedback experiences.
- An SEO migration map, accessibility checks and functional validation.
- A concise list of any business facts or operational claims still requiring confirmation.

Provide the redesign in a reviewable form appropriate to the task: high-fidelity page designs for a design engagement, or a working review version when implementation is requested. State which has been delivered. Do not describe a static mockup as a functioning site, and do not replace the live website without launch approval.

The final experience should feel calm, capable and personal. A visitor should quickly understand who I Care Group Services is, what support it offers, how it approaches care and how to take the next step.
